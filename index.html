Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay before the price is confirmed?

No. The intended workflow is to review the quote first. The order proceeds only after you agree to the quote and the payment step is confirmed.

Can I send a link from 1688 or Pinduoduo?

Yes. Share the product link and include the exact options and quantity you need. Some links or products may need additional verification.

Does the form place an order automatically?

No. This starter website prepares a request message. Ezih must review it, provide a quote, and confirm the next steps with you.

Can I track my order online?

This first version does not yet have private customer accounts or live tracking. Order updates are handled manually until a shared database and tracking dashboard are connected.
